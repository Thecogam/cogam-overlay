Privacy policy

Creator OS is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @thecogam. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's own permission it can upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and read that channel's videos and public statistics (views, likes, comments).

What it stores

Access tokens are kept on the owner's own computer, never shared or sold. No data about other people is collected from YouTube or Google.

Sharing

No Google user data is shared with, sold to, or used by anyone else. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
